Features

Innovative features.

📸

AI face analysis

Take or upload classroom photos. SnapClass checks each detected face against the selected subject’s roster and shows suggested matches for you to review.

🎙

Voice roll call

Record one enrolled student speaking. SnapClass compares the recording with saved voice profiles and lets you confirm the match.

▦

QR-powered enrollment

Share a subject by link, QR code, or join code. Review daily totals and individual attendance, or mark a student present manually.

Under the hood

Advanced Tech Stack

Streamlit & Flask

Streamlit runs the attendance portals. Flask serves this landing page and its interactive preview.

Face Registration

dlib detects faces and creates embeddings for student sign-in and group-photo attendance.

Voice Embeddings

Librosa processes recordings, and Resemblyzer creates embeddings for speaker matching.

Supabase Cloud

Postgres stores student profiles, subject enrollments, and daily attendance records.
